---

1. Direct Definition

Dukkha refers to unsatisfactoriness inherent in conditioned existence. It includes physical suffering, psychological distress, and the instability of all conditioned phenomena due to impermanence.


---

2. Interpretive Insight (Samma AI Synthesis)

Dukkha is not limited to pain. It describes the structural unreliability of all conditioned phenomena. Because all things arise dependent on causes, they are vulnerable to change and dissolution. Even pleasure carries instability. Understanding this shifts perception from surface emotion to existential insight.
